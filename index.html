Nararamdaman Mo Ba Ito?

02

Kung parent ka, nakikita mo ba ang anak mo na halos buong araw nakatutok sa screen, nawawalan ng focus, at parang kulang sa energy? O kung ikaw naman ang adult, nararamdaman mo ba ang stress, pagiging out of shape, at parang walang accessible na paraan para maging physically active?

Madalas, nahihirapan tayong humanap ng tamang environment para sa personal development. Lalo na kung ang hanap mo ay:

  • Kids na masyadong nakatutok sa gadgets: Palaging nasa phone o tablet, kulang sa physical activity at social interaction.
  • Kakulangan sa disiplina at focus: Hirap sundin ang instructions, madaling ma-distract, at walang self-control.
  • Adults na stressed at out of shape: Kailangan ng physical outlet at mental break pero walang affordable o convenient na gym.
  • Takot sa hindi kilalang martial arts school: Paano kung hindi qualified ang instructors? Paano kung hindi safe ang environment?
  • Naghahanap ng character-building, hindi lang fighting: Gusto mo ng lugar na huhubog sa buong pagkatao, hindi lang sa physical skills.

Sound familiar, diba? Hindi ka nag-iisa. Pero may solusyon.

About Us

30 Years of Martial Arts Excellence in PANGASINAN Welcome to JEV Eagle Hand Karate Do Club Dojo, located at Poblacion Montemayor St., Malasiqui, Philippines. For over three decades, we have been more than just a gym—we are a family institution dedicated to building character, strength, and discipline. Our dojo is led by Sensei Gerardo "Gerry" Valdez, a highly respected 7th Dan Black Belt with 30 years of teaching experience. He is joined by his brother, Ernest Valdez (5th Dan), and his son, Gerriemel Valdez, creating a unique multi-generational team of instructors who treat every student like family. Our walls are lined with trophies and medals earned by our students in local and national competitions—a testament to our proven training methods. …

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Magkano ang bayad sa Valdez Karate Dojo? A: Ang aming monthly fee ay ₱800 lamang. May one-time registration fee na 500 para sa new students. Walang hidden charges—affordable quality training for everyone! Q: Ano ang schedule ng classes? A: Bukas kami every Weekdays from 1:00 PM to 5:00 PM (perfect for after-school training) at Saturdays from 8:00 AM to 4:00 PM. Flexible ang schedule para sa mga estudyante at working adults. Q: Saan exact location ng dojo? A: Kami ay matatagpuan sa Poblacion Montemayor St., Malasiqui, Philippines 2421. Madali lang kami puntahan at may sapat na parking space. Q: Pwede bang magsimula kahit walang experience o kaya naman sobrang bata/matanda? A: Oo naman! Tinatanggap namin ang students mula age 6 pataas hanggang sa adults. Hindi kailangan ng prior experience. Ang aming instructors, led by Sensei Gerry (7th Dan), ay sanay magturo sa lahat ng level mula white belt hanggang black belt. Q: May trial class ba kayo? A: Yes! Inaanyayahan namin kayong dumaloy sa dojo para makita ang training firsthand. Maaari kang mag-observe o sumubok ng isang session bago mag-decide. Gusto naming siguraduhing comfortable ka sa aming environment. Q: Kailangan ba akong bumili ng uniform (Gi) agad? A: Para sa trial o first few classes, pwedeng mag-training clothes muna (t-shirt at jogging pants). Kapag nag-commit ka na, bibigyan ka namin ng advice kung saan makakakuha ng proper uniform.
