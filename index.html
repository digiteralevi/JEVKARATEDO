About Us

30 Years of Martial Arts Excellence in PANGASINAN Welcome to JEV Eagle Hand Karate Do Club Dojo, located at Poblacion Montemayor St., Malasiqui, Philippines. For over three decades, we have been more than just a gym—we are a family institution dedicated to building character, strength, and discipline. Our dojo is led by Sensei Gerardo "Gerry" Valdez, a highly respected 7th Dan Black Belt with 30 years of teaching experience. He is joined by his brother, Ernest Valdez (5th Dan), and his son, Gerriemel Valdez, creating a unique multi-generational team of instructors who treat every student like family. Our walls are lined with trophies and medals earned by our students in local and national competitions—a testament to our proven training methods. …

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Magkano ang bayad sa Valdez Karate Dojo? A: Ang aming monthly fee ay ₱800 lamang. May one-time registration fee na 500 para sa new students. Walang hidden charges—affordable quality training for everyone! Q: Ano ang schedule ng classes? A: Bukas kami every Weekdays from 1:00 PM to 5:00 PM (perfect for after-school training) at Saturdays from 8:00 AM to 4:00 PM. Flexible ang schedule para sa mga estudyante at working adults. Q: Saan exact location ng dojo? A: Kami ay matatagpuan sa Poblacion Montemayor St., Malasiqui, Philippines 2421. Madali lang kami puntahan at may sapat na parking space. Q: Pwede bang magsimula kahit walang experience o kaya naman sobrang bata/matanda? A: Oo naman! Tinatanggap namin ang students mula age 6 pataas hanggang sa adults. Hindi kailangan ng prior experience. Ang aming instructors, led by Sensei Gerry (7th Dan), ay sanay magturo sa lahat ng level mula white belt hanggang black belt. Q: May trial class ba kayo? A: Yes!
